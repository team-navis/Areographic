Areographic: NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 hackathon demonstration

This is a hackathon prototype, not a NASA product. No endorsement by NASA, JPL, Caltech or USGS is claimed or implied.
The 3D demonstration replays archived observations as bundled static assets. The independent control pad requests NASA Mars Trek map tiles at runtime; this is not a live Mars sensor feed. Habitat, crew state, oxygen, dose, alerts and mission time are simulated. Sampling-point positions are estimates, not official target coordinates. This project does not detect or judge life and performs no new instrument analysis.

MODELS AND PRESENTATION
Default astronaut: NASA 3D Resources, Mark III Spacesuit. Credit: NASA/Michael D. Carbajal.
https://science.nasa.gov/3d-resources/mark-iii-spacesuit/
The static Mark III model is a visual reference, not a claim of Mars surface certification or a simulated real suit design. The team removed the dedicated NASA insignia atlas and its embedded images while preserving all 14 mesh buffers.
Habitat, operator, crew rover, procedural objects, sky and presentation tinting: team-authored demonstration elements. The rover is fictional, not Perseverance and not a real vehicle design.
HUD design-kit images and control-pad logo: generated by the team with OpenAI image generation; source and processing records are retained in docs/REFERENCES.md and assets/hud/provenance.json.
Textures: Poly Haven, CC0 1.0. Individual source and author records: textures/polyhaven/LICENSES.json.
Removed EVOVA models are not included in this public repository or deployment.
